This page collects public-facing data and research tools that I maintain.

## Data and Tools

### [GradCafe Political Science PhD Results, 2016-2026](https://ye-rim-oh.github.io/GradCafe/)

An interactive dashboard of political science PhD admissions results reported on GradCafe, covering application cycles from 2016 to 2026.

### [Methods Hub](https://ye-rim-oh.github.io/methods-hub/)

A curated research methods reading hub for political science and related social science work.

### [Korean Party Policy Comparison](https://ye-rim-oh.github.io/kr-party-policy/)

An interactive tool comparing 48 policy questions across six major South Korean parties, with party matching based on users' responses. [Repository](https://github.com/ye-rim-oh/kr-party-policy).
